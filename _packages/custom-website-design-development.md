Custom Website Design & Development is for projects where the site needs a stronger visual identity, more deliberate content structure, or more tailored implementation than a standard launch package provides.

The package remains focused on a website, not an application. Projects involving significant data models, authenticated portals, advanced business logic, or complex system integrations move into Advanced Web Solutions.
