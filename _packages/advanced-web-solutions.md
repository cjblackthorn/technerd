Advanced Web Solutions covers projects that behave more like software than a traditional website.

Examples can include customer or client portals, authenticated experiences, databases, API integrations, advanced forms, custom e-commerce workflows, booking systems, dashboards, and specialized business logic. Final scope and price are established through discovery rather than forcing complex work into a fixed package.
