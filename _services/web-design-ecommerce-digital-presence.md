Your website should fit what you actually do.

TechNerd designs and builds professional websites for businesses, independent professionals, authors, creators, organizations, and e-commerce operations. Projects can range from straightforward representative sites and personal brands to Shopify stores, publishing platforms, and custom web solutions.

## Experience Beyond the Build

The work is informed by experience with more than page layouts and site builders. TechNerd brings practical experience with e-commerce operations, vendor coordination, customer and constituent support, technical administration, business processes, and long-term platform ownership.

That matters because a successful site is not only a visual project. It has to support the people who operate it and the people who use it.

Experience spans business and consulting sites, personal and professional brands, author and publishing properties, creative and band sites, community organizations, and e-commerce operations.

This service is not positioned as bargain website production or unlimited revisions for a flat fee. TechNerd scopes the work clearly, identifies third-party costs, builds for maintainability, and preserves the client's ownership of their business accounts, domains, content, and platform access.
