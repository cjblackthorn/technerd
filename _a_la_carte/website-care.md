Website Care is intended for routine maintenance, basic health review, and small content changes. It is not an unlimited development retainer.

Included monthly time does not roll over unless a written service agreement says otherwise. Larger changes, redesigns, new pages, integrations, incident recovery, or custom development are quoted separately or billed at the applicable hourly rate.

Third-party platform, hosting, domain, plugin, theme, application, and licensing fees remain the client's responsibility unless explicitly included.
